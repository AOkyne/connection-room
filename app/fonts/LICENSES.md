# Fonts

These fonts are served with the site (not downloaded from Google at build
time, which made builds fail intermittently). Copied from the Fontsource
npm packages; all are licensed under the SIL Open Font License 1.1.

- Cormorant Garamond (400, 500, 600, 700): @fontsource/cormorant-garamond
- Inter (variable weight): @fontsource-variable/inter
- Playfair Display (400-700, normal and italic): @fontsource/playfair-display
- Dancing Script (600, 700): @fontsource/dancing-script

Latin subset only, as before. Loaded in app/layout.tsx with next/font/local.
